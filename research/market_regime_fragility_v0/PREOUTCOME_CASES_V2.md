## Case C — 2026-02-02 — non-tech / resource-led stress

Contemporaneous close-time evidence:

- All major A-share indices fell more than 2%; fewer than 800 stocks advanced and more than 4,600 declined.
- Turnover contracted to about CNY 2.59tn, but the day featured broad price damage rather than a quiet low-liquidity drift.
- Cyclicals/resources (non-ferrous, steel, chemicals, coal, oil-related exposures) led losses after a severe global precious-metals / commodity repricing.
- Defensive receiving side was narrow: food/beverage and banks were only modestly positive.
- Contemporaneous reporting described external macro repricing, precious-metal crashes, seasonal liquidity tightening, ETF redemptions / profit-taking and a multi-sell feedback loop.
- Technology also weakened, but the initiating stress was not an AI/technology-only event.

Pre-outcome state:

`EXTERNAL_SHOCK` + `ACTIVE_DE_RISKING`

Pre-outcome severity:

`SEVERITY_HIGH`

Reasoning:

This case combines extreme breadth damage, cross-asset stress, defensive-only rotation and broad price weakness. Contracting turnover prevents using volume expansion as a required condition; severity is instead supported by the breadth/cross-asset/liquidity evidence stack.

## Case D — 2026-07-06 — broad weakness with multiple receiving themes

Contemporaneous close-time evidence:

- Shanghai -0.06%, Shenzhen -1.16%, ChiNext -1.77%; more than 3,500 stocks declined.
- Turnover was about CNY 3.09tn, down roughly CNY 91bn from the prior session: liquidity remained high in absolute terms but was not expanding.
- Weakness was concentrated in parts of high-beta hardware/PCB, while several independent receiving themes remained active: medicine, coal, liquid cooling, switches/networking, cultivated diamonds, pork.
- The market therefore showed dispersion and rotation rather than a single-direction broad liquidation.
- No independently verified cross-asset shock is assigned for this case.

Pre-outcome state:

`INTERNAL_FRAGILITY_WATCH`

Pre-outcome severity:

`SEVERITY_LOW`

Reasoning:

Breadth was weak and growth indices underperformed, but market functioning remained orderly and multiple independent themes still attracted capital. Under the preregistered framework this should reduce chase tolerance, not trigger a broad high-beta shutdown.

- Case D: a fast repair supports the usefulness of the WATCH/LOW distinction; a severe continued decline indicates that the low-severity classification missed hidden stress.
